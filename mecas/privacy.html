MECAS — Privacy policy

Itsu Studio Apps · effective 26 November 2026

FrançaisEnglish

In short. MECAS runs on your device. We, the publisher, receive none of your data: no account with us, no server of ours, no ads, no analytics, no trackers. The app only talks to the services you ask it to reach.

What the app reads on your device

  • The audio files on your device ("Music and audio" permission), to list them, play them, compute their fingerprint and, when you ask, rename or sort them. Android asks for your consent before a file is renamed or moved.

What the app keeps on your device

  • Your catalogue of works, your forms and your settings.
  • Your SACEM and MusicStart session tokens, so you don't have to sign in again: in the app's private storage on a phone, in the system keychain (Windows, macOS) on a computer. Your password is never there: you type it yourself on SACEM's page, and MECAS never sees it.
  • A technical log (addresses of the services reached, file names, errors), which contains no password and no token. It only leaves your device if you send it yourself.

What the app sends, and only when you ask

  • To SACEM (createursediteurs.sacem.fr, wm-services.sacem.fr): sign-in to your member account, then the work registrations you approve (titles, participants, shares, duration), and reading your profile (name, member number, email address) to prefill your forms.
  • To MusicStart (musicstart.com and its services hosted by Amazon Web Services in Ireland): sign-in through your SACEM account, then the audio files, lyrics and details of the works you choose to deposit there.
  • To AcoustID (acoustid.org), only if you entered an AcoustID key in the settings: the acoustic fingerprint of a track (computed on its first 120 seconds) and its duration, to check that it doesn't match a recording that already exists. The audio file itself is not sent.

These services handle that data under their own privacy policies. Every connection is encrypted (HTTPS).

Demo mode

Demo mode shows the app with made-up works and accounts. It reads none of your files and sends nothing.

Google Play

If you install MECAS from Google Play, Google may collect crash reports and install statistics under its own policies. We see them anonymised in the Play Console, to fix errors.

Sharing and selling

We don't sell, rent or share any data: we don't receive any.

Age

MECAS is made for music authors, composers and publishers. It is not meant for anyone under 18.

Deleting your data

Everything MECAS keeps is on your device. You can delete a work in the app, sign out (which erases the tokens) or uninstall the app (which erases everything on Android). For data deposited with SACEM or MusicStart, contact those services.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes too, and the release notes say so.
